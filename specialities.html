Comprehensive Multi Speciality Services

We offer specialized medical and surgical services led by senior doctors and backed by modern technology.

Cardiology & Heart Care

Orthopedics & Joint Care

Advanced trauma surgery, joint replacement (Knee & Hip), spine care, fracture management, and arthroscopic procedures.

  • Fracture & Trauma Care

Neurology & Neurosurgery

Expert care for stroke, epilepsy, migraine, neuropathy, brain injuries, and spine conditions with 24/7 ICU support.

  • Stroke Management Unit
  • Spine & Brain Surgery

  • Hernia & Appendectomy

  • Infertility & Women Health
